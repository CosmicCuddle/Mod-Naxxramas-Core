**Additional hook distinction:** `OnPlayerLootItem` appears on successful `Player::StoreLootItem`, while direct group Need/Greed awards use `OnPlayerGroupRollRewardItem`; master-loot assignment calls `StoreNewItem` directly. A full-bag mail-on-roll result calls `SendRollWonItemViaMail` instead and does not emit that direct roll-award hook. Therefore **a single packet filter or a single success observer cannot claim comprehensive coverage**. The fact that an entrypoint lacks a second `AllowedForPlayer` check is not in itself proof of an invalid real award. Confirm all actual registered hook implementations before proposing a global diagnostic.

## Quest-item status is a SOURCE PROPERTY, not the name of a quest

- `Loot/LootMgr.cpp:395–466` constructs a `LootItem` with `needs_quest` from the **effective generated loot row**, and `ITEM_FLAG_MULTI_DROP` sets `freeforall` per template. Do not confuse group `FREE_FOR_ALL` loot method with `MULTI_DROP`.
- `LootItem::AllowedForPlayer` checks conditions, faction, recipes, disables, quest starter restrictions and `needs_quest && !HasQuestForItem`. Crucially, `ITEM_FLAGS_CU_IGNORE_QUEST_STATUS` can bypass quest-status rules. Other hooks also participate. `QuestRequired=0` can be a normal one-copy shared item EVEN IF a quest objective.
- `Loot::FillQuestLoot` at `Loot/LootMgr.cpp:658–691` creates per-player quest slots after eligibility, with deliberate exception for the master looter's listing (not an unrestricted award). `Loot::LootItemInSlot` at 821–878 maps each player's own quest slot list; ordinary normal slots and shared drops are a different path.
- A **friend without a quest** taking an ordinary `QuestRequired=0` item on their legitimate turn is not automatically a bug. A **friend eligible for the same quest** taking one normal shared drop is also not automatically a bug. A `MULTI_DROP` personal copy is not stealing the other person's drop.

**Peer assessment:** [CS03 review of original #150 exact `cca8ea9`](https://github.com/CosmicCuddle/Mod-Naxxramas-Core/pull/150#issuecomment-6399573014) confirmed the core source claims and supplied corrections. **Important source distinction:** a *real* `needs_quest` / `quest_items` row participates in a group roll under the `ITEM_FLAGS_CU_FOLLOW_LOOT_RULES` custom-flag condition. A quest-related **ordinary** (`QuestRequired=0`) row can roll under ordinary group rules regardless of that flag; the exact item source, `FlagsCustom`, `MaxCount`, required objective count and simultaneous drops are necessary for an honest diagnosis. In `CountTheRoll` the Need/Greed call to `CanStoreNewItem` occurs at pinned **L1655/L1739**, and the direct `StoreNewItem` at **L1662/L1746**. Eligibility might change after roll admission; source-possible does NOT prove the reported human friend's award was invalid.

**Mail is default-off in pinned source but not verified in owner config.** A `MaxCount` failure is **not** `EQUIP_ERR_INVENTORY_FULL`; mail is **not** automatic on unique/max-count errors. A recorded roll winner unable to store may leave the item blocked/claimed rather than awarding it; do not confuse a stranded slot with a bag grant.

**Modified-client vote case is security research, not the current gameplay hypothesis.** `Group::CountRollVote` can accept a second vote or an auto-passed player's crafted vote without checking prior vote state; a real unmodified client is not offered that choice. Nothing here recommends a live packet experiment or a broad loot redesign.
